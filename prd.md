## Product Vision

BizBuddy is an all-in-one digital business solutions platform that helps
startups, local businesses, professionals, and entrepreneurs grow
through digital marketing, accounting, websites, branding, and business
support services.

The website should function as a lead-generation platform, online
service catalog, quotation system, and future client portal.
